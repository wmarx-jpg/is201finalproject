Dr. Pepper brings back creamy coconut flavor

The drink first debuted two years ago and is tied to summer

Published: April 15, 2026 at 11:03 AM
Tags: Dr. Pepper

Dr. Pepper is bringing back its creamy coconut flavor for a limited time.

The drink first debuted two years ago and is tied to summer.

Recommended Videos

It’s inspired by the “dirty Dr. Pepper” trend, which some fans make at home by adding lime juice, coconut syrup and cream to regular Dr. Pepper.

That ought to play well in Roanoke, which has been called the Dr. Pepper Capital of the World.

The company chose the city to build its first bottling plant east of the Mississippi River back in 1936.
